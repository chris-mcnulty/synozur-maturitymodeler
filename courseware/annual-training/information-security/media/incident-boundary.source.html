WHEN SOMETHING HAS HAPPENED
Your part and the responders’ part

You: every employee

Act quickly, stick to the facts
Stop

Stop interacting with the suspicious content or activity.

Report

Share what happened, when, and what may be affected.

Preserve

Keep messages, timestamps, and details. Don’t delete anything.

Follow

Follow responders’ instructions and share details only with them.

Authorized responders

Contain, investigate, decide
Contain

End sessions, reset credentials, or isolate devices.

Investigate

Work out what happened and how far it reached.

Notify?

Decide with legal advisers whether anyone must be notified.

Coordinate

Handle any contact with clients or other outside parties.

Leave to responders: wiping devices · revisiting suspicious sites · confronting anyone · contacting clients or authorities about an incident
