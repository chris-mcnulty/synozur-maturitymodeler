WHEN A REQUEST PUTS PRESSURE ON YOU
Pause, verify independently, report
1

Pause

Does it involve urgency, secrecy, sign-in codes, payments, software, or moving data? Slow down before you act.

2

Verify independently

Use a contact you already know: the company directory, a saved number, or the published IT support address. Never the details in the request.

CONFIRMED AND EXPECTED

Continue

Proceed through the normal, approved process.

CAN’T CONFIRM, OR ALREADY ACTED

3 · Report

Don’t act on the request. Report what happened and when, and keep the message. You don’t need proof.

Already clicked, replied, or approved? Go straight to Report. Early reports limit harm.
Use this for any request involving access, codes, payments, software, or data, by email, chat, phone, QR code, or video.
