## 3.3 Practice: A client transcript and a new AI tool

Practice, not graded (passing score 0).

### 1. Before any part of this transcript goes into the new tool, who should assess whether that use is allowed?

| | Choice | Feedback |
|---|---|---|
| A | You can decide, because the names are already removed. | Removing names doesn’t settle it: the project and pricing details are still client information, and the new purpose and tool need review. |
| B | The AI tool’s vendor, through its terms of service. | Vendor terms describe how the vendor handles uploads, not whether the client or Synozur permits this use. |
| C ✓ | The engagement lead and the responsible data owner, who check the client’s restrictions and whether the tool is approved for this use. | They can confirm the client’s instructions, the purpose, and the tool’s approval for this data. |
| D | A teammate who already uses the tool for similar work. | Someone else’s use doesn’t show the tool is approved for this client, data, or purpose. |

- **Correct:** C
- **Learner sees after submitting:** Removing names leaves the unique project and pricing details, vendor terms don’t grant client permission, and a colleague’s habit isn’t an approval. The engagement lead and the responsible data owner check the client’s restrictions, the purpose, and the tool’s approval before anything is uploaded. Revisit “AI and vendors need boundaries.”

## 4.3 Practice: A deletion request during a legal hold

Practice, not graded (passing score 0).

### 1. The person asks you to confirm today that their feedback has been deleted. What do you tell them?

| | Choice | Feedback |
|---|---|---|
| A | That it has been deleted, and plan to delete it once the hold ends. | That would be untrue, and records under a hold must not be deleted or changed without direction. |
| B ✓ | That you’ve recorded their request and passed it to the people who handle these requests, who will follow up. | An honest acknowledgment with no promises; authorized owners assess the request and the hold. |
| C | That the request is refused because of the legal hold. | Deciding how the hold affects the request, and what the person is told, is for the privacy/legal owner and the legal-hold contact. |
| D | A specific date when their information will be erased. | You can’t promise timing or complete erasure before the request is assessed. |

- **Correct:** B
- **Learner sees after submitting:** Claiming deletion would be untrue, refusing decides a question that isn’t yours, and a date is a promise nobody has assessed. Acknowledge the request, record it, and route it. The privacy/legal owner and the legal-hold contact decide how the hold and the request interact and what the person is told. Revisit “Route requests and report disclosures.”
- **Revisit after an incorrect response:** Route requests and report disclosures
- **Source:** Prompt 2: hold and deletion request (who assesses; what to tell the person)

### 1. Which information may be confidential even when it contains no personal data?

| | Choice | Feedback |
|---|---|---|

| B | Only employee medical information. | Medical information concerns people, but confidential business information can contain no personal data. |

- **Correct:** C

### 2. A task needs job role and region. You can export a full personnel file. What is best?

| | Choice | Feedback |
|---|---|---|
